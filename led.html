PRECISION FITMENT GUIDE

Vehicle Compatibility

Engineered For Standard 74mm & 82mm Emblems • Universal Adaptation

BMW

BMW Dedicated Fitment

Direct OEM-style replacement for standard BMW roundel emblems measuring 82mm (typically front bonnet / hood, and select rear trunks) and 74mm (typically rear trunk lids). Seamless clip/adhesive flush mounting without modifying factory bodywork.

✨ NO DRILLING REQUIRED: Designed to utilize original factory routing access. Zero body panel modifications or hole drilling required.
⚠️ Important Compatibility Notice: Vehicles with next-generation integrated rear cameras, washer nozzles, or active electronic sensors built directly inside the emblem housing are NOT compatible. Please inspect your factory emblem setup prior to commissioning.
Available Sizes: 82mm / 74mm
UNI

Non-BMW Universal Conversion

Fully adaptable to all non-BMW vehicles with simple custom wire routing through existing emblem pinholes.

💡 Flat-Surface Requirement: Ensure the mounting surface on your vehicle panel is completely FLAT once the factory badge is removed. As long as the base is flat, select the 82mm version to achieve a seamless, perfectly flush fitment.
Recommended Variant: 82mm Flat Mount

04 / Preventive Care & Essential Cautions

High-Pressure Washdown Warning

While the enclosure features IP68/IP69 weather sealing, avoid direct, prolonged exposure to high-pressure waterjets at point-blank range during vehicle washdowns to prevent premature sealing gasket aging.

Front Hood PPF Film Armor

For front-hood installations, we highly recommend applying Paint Protection Film (PPF) over the optical badge surface for maximum shielding against road debris impacts, stone chips, and direct UV degradation.
